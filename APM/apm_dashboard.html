ADQS – Asset Data Quality Score (0-100) = average of 5 dimensions. Can we trust this asset's data before using it for health or investment decisions?
Completeness data is there · Validity follows rules / allowed values · Accuracy agrees with its source (proxy checks: last reading, outliers, labour hours) · Consistency same fact agrees across tables · Timeliness recent enough to trust. Bands: ≥80 Trusted, 60-79 Usable, <60 Fix data first.

APM Health – Asset Health Index (AHI, 0-100, 100 = perfect). 35% Failure history (each corrective/emergency WO -15) + 25% PM compliance (PMs not overdue) + 25% Condition (latest meter reading vs its average) + 15% Backlog (each open WO -10). Bands: ≥80 Good, 60-79 Fair, 40-59 Poor, <40 Critical.

ACI – Asset Criticality Index (0-100). How much it hurts if this asset fails. 50% Priority (1 = 100 … 5 = 20) + 30% Maintenance cost exposure (vs costliest asset) + 20% Failure frequency (vs asset with most CM/EM WOs). Bands: ≥70 High, 40-69 Medium, <40 Low.

Health × Criticality. Poor health = AHI < 60 · High criticality = ACI ≥ 60. Each quadrant gets its own maintenance and investment strategy. Assets with ADQS < 60 are flagged Verify data before acting.

AIP – Asset Investment Planning. Risk = (100 − AHI) × ACI / 100. Action: AHI<40 & ACI≥60 → Replace (Year 1) · AHI<60 → Refurbish (Year 1-2) · AHI<80 & ACI≥60 → Increase inspection (Year 2-3) · else Run & monitor. Confidence comes from ADQS — low ADQS means fix the data before committing budget.
